----------------------------------------------------------------------------
                             M E G A W A D   2 
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Megawad 2 is 30 of the best Deathmatch levels in the world, compiled just
for you.  Aren't you special.  This 4 Megabyte WAD includes all new music,
sound effects, and some cool new graphics.  The levels are divided into 3
sections, so you can easily "-warp" to any of the levels when you are
starting a deathmatch.  Levels 1-14 are fast deathmatch levels, 15-24 are
just some of the AWESOME levels made by Megasphere members, and 25-30 are
special levels and team games.

These levels are designed to be played in new deathmatch mode "-altdeath"
In many levels, using a different skill level will change the types of
weapons on the level.

IMPORTANT: See "Rules.TXT" for info on how to play the team games!
The text files we could find for included levels are in "TEXTS.ZIP".
